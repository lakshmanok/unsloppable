# Figure 1-1: Ambient Music Experience Prompts

## Final Approved Prompt (Iteration 2: Strict Monochrome Ink)

```text
A strictly monochrome single-strip three-panel editorial cartoon in pure black ink on clean off-white cream paper, blending James Thurber, RK Laxman, and Saul Steinberg.

Strict style rule: NO colors whatsoever, zero color fills, pure black dip-pen line art on off-white paper with generous empty negative space.

Three side-by-side panels comparing ambient piano music experiences:
Panel 1: "Hotel Lobby" - RK Laxman's Common Man (elderly, round wire spectacles, balding with white tufts over ears, rumpled checkered coat) walking past a self-playing upright player piano with moving keys and empty bench, staring in bewildered fascination.
Panel 2: "Restaurant" - The same flummoxed everyman walking past a quiet grand piano where a well-dressed pianist in a dark suit is playing softly and delicately.
Panel 3: "Shopping Mall" - The everyman bewildered with eyes wide and hands slightly raised as a wild, flamboyant visitor with unruly hair and coattails vigorously pounds on an upright public piano in theatrical frenzy.

Minimalist architectural line economy in the style of Saul Steinberg. Figures with James Thurber's soft, organic humanism and gentle contours. Completely black and white ink line drawing.

Signed "Unsloppable" in delicate black ink script in the lower right corner.
Centered at the bottom beneath the panels in clean New Yorker serif typography:
"It ranges from mildly haunted, to quietly civilized, to a hostage situation."
```

## Initial Candidate Prompt (Iteration 1)

```text
A minimalist three-panel horizontal editorial cartoon strip drawn in black ink on clean off-white cream paper, blending the artistic styles of James Thurber, RK Laxman, and Saul Steinberg. 

The cartoon is divided into three distinct side-by-side panels comparing ambient piano music experiences, with RK Laxman's iconic Common Man everyman (an elderly, modest gentleman with round wire-rim spectacles, balding crown with wispy tufts of hair over his ears, and a rumpled checkered coat) appearing in each panel:

Panel 1 (Hotel Lobby): The flummoxed everyman walks into a quiet hotel lobby, staring in quiet bafflement at an eerie automated player piano whose keys are pressing themselves with nobody on the bench.
Panel 2 (Fine Restaurant): The everyman walks into a dimly lit restaurant where a well-dressed pianist in a dark suit softly and delicately plays a grand piano, creating a dignified, gentle mood.
Panel 3 (Public Mall): The flummoxed everyman walks into a public shopping mall atrium, bewildered and overwhelmed as an exuberant, flamboyant visitor dramatically slams and pounds on a brightly colored public street piano with wild, theatrical gestures and flying elbows.

Artistic Style: Pure black dip-pen line art with Saul Steinberg's sparse conceptual lines and generous negative space, infused with James Thurber's soft, vulnerable, organic human contours. Flat linework, no 3D rendering, no digital gradients, no color fills.

Signature: Neatly signed "Unsloppable" in delicate cartoonist ink script in the lower right corner.

Caption: Centered neatly at the bottom beneath the panels in clean New Yorker style serif typography:
"It ranges from mildly haunted, to quietly civilized, to a hostage situation."
```
