# fig8-5 — Personal responsibility

## Caption

“The reactor has a backup system; your responsibility doesn’t.”

The caption is invented cartoon dialogue, not a historical quotation.

## Panel

Admiral Rickover holds a reactor safety notice reading “PERSONALLY RESPONSIBLE: YOU,” pointing toward the large word YOU. Across the desk sits a bewildered civilian engineer with round spectacles and a loose checkered jacket. A small reactor console provides context. Rounded black ink contours, simplified detail, and a white background soften the figures. The lower-right corner bears the signature “Unsloppable.”
