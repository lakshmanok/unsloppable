## Scene Description

- **The Setting**: A minimalist office drawn with Saul Steinberg's spare line economy.
- **The Conflict**: The Common Man sits behind his desk, comically overwhelmed and buried up to his chest in a suffocating mountain of continuous accordion-folded computer paper emblazoned with `AI SLOP`. Perched right on top of the pile is an imposing mechanical pyramid metronome vigorously ticking back and forth with sharp motion lines.
- **The Antagonist**: A sleek, smiling corporate executive stands beside the desk in a crisp business suit, open-handed and beaming with pride at the "cost efficiency" of the robotic setup.
- **The Missing Soul**: Pushed aside into the dusty corner is a complete jazz drum set—the symbol of human timbre, rubato, groove, and living musical expression that was discarded in favor of mindless mechanical consistency.
