Better work. Better workdays.

Make your point.
Make their day.

A clear recommendation. A presentation that gets to the point. An email your colleagues can act on. Good communication makes everyone’s work a little easier.

This book is for anyone who writes emails, reports, proposals, or presentations and wants to use AI well. Learn to question its suggestions, bring your own experience, and turn a plausible draft into something worth reading.

Meet the author

Valliappa
LakshmananYou can call me Lak.

An AI practitioner who cares about the human doing the thinking.

Dr. Valliappa (“Lak”) Lakshmanan is co-founder and CTO of Obin, building deep domain AI agents for finance. Previously, he led Data Analytics and AI Solutions on Google Cloud and worked as a research scientist at NOAA.

He co-founded Google’s Advanced Solutions Lab and has written books, research papers, and Coursera courses on AI. He is a Fellow of the American Meteorological Society for pioneering the use of machine learning and AI in severe weather prediction.
