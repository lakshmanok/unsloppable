Center an elderly balding man with white side tufts, round wire spectacles, rumpled checkered jacket, mild bewilderment, seated at a tiny desk looking at an open laptop. ## Candidate 2 — kinder concept

Create an original square single-panel editorial cartoon for the book Unsloppable. Tone must be deeply kind, humanistic, warm, gently funny. Never blame or ridicule the human. Concept: An elderly gentle everyman with balding crown, soft white side tufts, round wire spectacles and a rumpled checkered coat sits at a simple desk. He smiles affectionately while holding a tiny handwritten card with just the clearly legible words "Thank you." Beside him an open laptop has produced an absurdly long, ornate scroll that spills over the desk and curls gently across the floor; the scroll bears only a few tiny illegible text lines and ornamental flourishes, NO readable jargon. The joke is the machine overcomplicating a simple sincere expression. The human is quietly wise, tender, slightly bemused, never helpless or foolish. Blend James Thurber soft humanistic unpretentious contours, R.K. Laxman elderly everyday observer with spectacles and checkered coat, Saul Steinberg extremely spare conceptual dip-pen line economy. Pure black ink outlines on clean warm white paper, lots of negative space, no color, no gradients, no 3D, almost no hatching. Center below drawing a perfectly legible classic italic serif caption, with visible opening and closing quotation marks, EXACTLY: "I think she’ll like the short version." Sign "Unsloppable" in legible delicate handwritten black ink in the lower right of the drawing above the caption. Square composition, generous margins, gentle editorial wit, publication-quality.

## Candidate 3 — simplification prompt

Revise this cartoon preserving the kindly smiling elderly man, his round glasses, bald crown with side tufts, checkered coat, small card saying "Thank you.", laptop emitting absurdly long ornate scroll, exact caption “I think she’ll like the short version.” with quotation marks, italic serif typography, and Unsloppable signature. IMPORTANT: simplify drastically into spare black pen outlines on pure white, minimalist editorial cartoon with vast empty space. Remove ALL room scenery: window, pictures, plant, shelves, dog, cup, stationery and spare chair. Keep ONLY man on simple chair, small desk, laptop, tiny thank-you note, flowing scroll. Remove ALL shading, crosshatching, wood grain, floor markings, black fills in trousers; coat check pattern should have just a few widely spaced lines. Draw scroll with just a few tiny marks and two small flourishes. Soft affectionate smile, kindly human dignity, gentle warmth. No color or gradients. Fewer lines, more air. Square composition.
