## Caption

“Let this 6 go—the express 4 is right behind it, and we’ll get there faster.”

## Panel

A relaxed artist woman stops her hurried finance-bro partner from boarding the 6 in a NYC subway station. The arrival board says “4 EXPRESS 1 MIN.” Inside the open doorway, the elderly Everyman grips a pole, wearing round spectacles and a checkered coat; his crestfallen expression suggests he has just realized he chose the slower train. Signed “Unsloppable.”
