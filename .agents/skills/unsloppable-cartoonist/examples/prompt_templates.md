# Prompt Templates: Unsloppable Cartoon Recipes

Use these templates to construct high-yield prompts for `generate_image`. Each template balances the 5 core characteristics.

---

## Anatomy of the Master Prompt

Every prompt for the `unsloppable-cartoonist` skill should follow this structured formula:

```text
A minimalist single-panel editorial cartoon in the combined style of James Thurber, RK Laxman, and Saul Steinberg.

[SUBJECT & COMPOSITION]:
Centered in the composition is RK Laxman's signature "Common Man" everyman: an elderly, modest man with a balding crown and wispy white hair over his ears, wearing round wire-rim spectacles, a slightly rumpled checkered tweed coat, and simple trousers. He stands with a gently flummoxed, bewildered expression [SPECIFIC ACTION / SCENARIO].

[ARTISTIC STYLE & LINEWORK]:
Drawn with Saul Steinberg's spare, conceptual black dip-pen lines on a clean off-white cream paper background, featuring vast uncluttered negative space and minimal architectural strokes. Infused with James Thurber's whimsical, vulnerable, and soft organic humanism. Pure line art, flat, no 3D rendering, no volumetric shading, no digital gradients, no color fills.

[SIGNATURE]:
In the bottom-right corner of the drawing, it is signed "Unsloppable" in delicate, authentic cartoonist ink script.

[CAPTION]:
Centered cleanly at the bottom beneath the illustration, printed in crisp, elegant New Yorker-style italic serif typeface:
"[EXACT CAPTION TEXT]"
```

## Thematic Recipes

### 1. Technology & Artificial Intelligence
**Scenario**: The everyman being presented with an absurd AI solution.
```text
A minimalist single-panel editorial cartoon in the combined style of James Thurber, RK Laxman, and Saul Steinberg. Centered in the frame is RK Laxman's bewildered Common Man—an elderly balding gentleman with tufts of white hair, round wire-rim glasses, and a rumpled checkered jacket—staring with quiet perplexity at a sleek, hyper-minimalist black pedestal with a single blinking wire labeled "AI Strategist." Drawn with Saul Steinberg's spare, razor-sharp dip-pen line economy on clean cream paper with ample negative space, blended with James Thurber's gentle, organic, vulnerable human curves. Pure black ink line drawing, no shading, no gradients, no color. Signed "Unsloppable" in fine ink script in the lower right corner. At the bottom, centered in clean New Yorker italic serif typography, the caption reads:
"It doesn't actually solve the problem, but it generates fifty pages explaining why it was insoluble anyway."
```

---

### 2. Corporate Bureaucracy & Governance
**Scenario**: The everyman navigating baffling organizational hierarchy.
```text
A minimalist single-panel editorial cartoon blending the styles of James Thurber, RK Laxman, and Saul Steinberg. Centered is RK Laxman's flummoxed Common Man with round spectacles, balding head with wispy side hair, and a rumpled plaid coat, standing before a comically elaborate flow-chart drawn on a wall with Saul Steinberg's conceptual architectural lines. The chart has dozens of looping arrows all leading back to a small box labeled "Pending More Meetings." Infused with James Thurber's soft, loose, unpretentious human contours. Pure black line art on off-white paper, zero 3D shading, zero color. Signed "Unsloppable" in the bottom corner. Centered beneath in classic New Yorker italic serif lettering:
"We find that by reorganizing every six months, no one ever has time to realize we have no strategy."
```

### 3. Software Engineering & Systems
**Scenario**: Code refactoring, complexity, or "slop".
```text
A minimalist single-panel editorial cartoon in the style of James Thurber, RK Laxman, and Saul Steinberg. Centered is RK Laxman's bewildered everyman in his classic rumpled checkered coat and round spectacles, gently scratching his head while observing a bizarre mechanical contraption made of two delicate sticks holding up a grand, floating geometric cube. The drawing uses Saul Steinberg's sparse conceptual pen-and-ink lines and clean negative space, combined with James Thurber's warm, whimsical humanism. Simple black ink lines on warm cream paper, completely flat without shading or color. Signed "Unsloppable" in the lower right. Centered at the bottom in crisp New Yorker italic serif type:
"Don't touch that line—nobody knows what it does, but if we delete it the ceiling collapses."
```

### 4. Modern Daily Life & Convenience
**Scenario**: Modern appliances overcomplicating simple tasks.
```text
A minimalist single-panel editorial cartoon combining James Thurber's humanistic simplicity, RK Laxman's flummoxed everyman, and Saul Steinberg's spare line art. Centered is RK Laxman's Common Man, wearing his distinctive round wire glasses and rumpled checkered jacket, looking completely baffled at a minimalist modern kitchen toaster featuring a digital dashboard with thirty buttons and a glowing update screen. Drawn in Saul Steinberg's clean, minimalist ink lines on white paper with vast negative space, with Thurber's gentle, endearing human contours. No color, no shading, black ink on white. Signed "Unsloppable" in the bottom right corner. Centered caption at the bottom in elegant italic serif font:
"It refused to toast my sourdough until I agreed to the revised terms of service."
```
